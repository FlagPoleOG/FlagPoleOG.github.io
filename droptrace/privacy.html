Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

DropTrace is built to work without collecting your data. It has no account, ads, analytics, or telemetry, and Second Shift Software doesn't receive anything from the app.

What stays on your PC

DropTrace saves outage times, likely causes, your router's local IP address, the time it spent monitoring, and your settings. This stays on your PC until you choose Delete history in Settings. Depending on how DropTrace was installed, uninstalling it may not remove this data.

If you create a report, the name, provider, and account number you type go only into the PDF. DropTrace doesn't save them. You decide who receives the file.

What connects to the internet

To test your connection, DropTrace contacts:

  • Your router.
  • Public servers at 1.1.1.1 (Cloudflare), 8.8.8.8 (Google), and 9.9.9.9 (Quad9), using pings and sometimes an encrypted connection test that sends no personal data.
  • Your configured DNS service, to look up www.microsoft.com, www.cloudflare.com, and dns.google.

As with any internet connection, these services can see your public IP address and when the connection was made. Their own privacy policies apply.
