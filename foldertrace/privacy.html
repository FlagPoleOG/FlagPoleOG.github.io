Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

FolderTrace works entirely on your PC. It has no account, ads, analytics, or telemetry, and it never connects to the internet. Second Shift Software doesn't receive anything from the app.

What FolderTrace reads

To work out which program each folder belongs to, FolderTrace reads, on your PC only:

  • The names, sizes, and dates of folders and files in AppData and Program Files.
  • Windows' list of installed programs, Microsoft Store apps, services, startup items, and shortcuts.
  • Windows' record of programs you've run, and the programs running right now.
  • Game library information from Steam, Epic, and GOG that's already on your PC.
  • Small log, crash, and settings files that may say which program made a folder.

It never opens browser data, passwords, or files in folders tied to logins, cookies, or sessions. For those folders it only adds up their size.

What stays on your PC

FolderTrace saves two things in its own folder: your Right and Wrong marks on its labels, and a list of program folders it found on your drives, so it doesn't have to search every time. Uninstalling FolderTrace may not remove this folder.

If you choose Save report, FolderTrace writes a CSV file where you pick. You decide who sees it.

Removing folders

FolderTrace only removes folders you check and confirm, and it moves them to the Recycle Bin so you can restore them. Scanning never changes anything.
