How to log outages for your provider

Support teams hear "my internet keeps cutting out" all day. A short, dated log makes your case harder to brush off.

What to record for each drop

  • The date and the time it started.
  • How long it lasted.
  • What stopped working: everything, one device, or only some websites.
  • Whether you were on Wi-Fi or a cable.
  • What the lights on your modem and router showed.
  • The results of the ping tests from this guide, if you had time to run them.

Rule out your own gear first

Before you call, restart the modem and router once, try a different cable, and test with a wired connection if you can. Tell support you did this. It skips the first round of scripted advice.

Keep it up for a week or two

One bad evening is easy to dismiss. Ten drops across two weeks, with times and lengths, shows a pattern. Patterns are what get a technician visit or a line check.

When you contact them

  • Give the number of drops, the date range and the typical length.
  • Say whether it happens at certain times of day.
  • Ask for a ticket number and for the line to be tested.
  • Ask whether they can see signal problems or errors on their side.
